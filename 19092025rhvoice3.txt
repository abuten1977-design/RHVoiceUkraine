# RHVoice Build Log - 19.09.2025

## Overall Goal
Compile the RHVoice speech synthesis engine with Ukrainian voices on a macOS machine.

## Key Knowledge
- The user's primary goal is to build RHVoice with Ukrainian voices (`anatol`, `natalia`, etc.).
- The build environment is a modern macOS machine with Homebrew installed at `/opt/homebrew`.
- The RHVoice build system is old and has numerous compatibility issues with macOS, requiring multiple patches to the `CMakeLists.txt` files.
- Standard CMake variables like `CMAKE_PREFIX_PATH` and `CMAKE_LINKER_FLAGS` are not being respected by the build scripts.
- A custom linker workaround script (`clangLinkerWorkaround.sh`) was found to be broken and was preventing executables from being created.

## Recent Actions
- Successfully compiled the `libRHVoice_core`, `libRHVoice_audio`, and `libRHVoice` libraries, and all executables including `RHVoice-test`.
- The build previously failed at the final linking stage for the `RHVoice-test` executable with the error `ld: library 'ao' not found`. This was resolved by adding `link_directories("/opt/homebrew/lib")` to the root `CMakeLists.txt`.
- The `cmake` configuration also failed due to an outdated `cmake_minimum_required` in `cmake/thirdParty/CCache/CMakeLists.txt`, which was resolved by updating it to `VERSION 3.5`.
- `RHVoice-test` initially failed with "No language resources are available". This was resolved by manually copying language and voice data from `RHVoice/data/` to `RHVoice/build/data/` and setting `RHVOICE_DATA_PATH` and `RHVOICE_CONFIG_PATH` environment variables.
- The `RHVoice-test` executable then ran successfully with `Exit Code: 0`.

## Build Attempts and Issues
- Initial `xcodebuild` failed due to missing `Info.plist` for `RHVoiceApp` and `RHVoiceFramework`, and missing umbrella header for `RHVoiceFramework`. These were addressed by creating the respective files and updating `Project.yml`.

This was attempted to be fixed by changing `deploymentTarget` to `iOS: "17.0"` in `Project.yml` and explicitly specifying `OS=17.0` in the `xcodebuild` command.
- The latest `xcodebuild` attempt failed because `xcrun simctl list devices` and `xcrun simctl list runtimes` returned empty lists, indicating no iOS Simulators or runtimes are installed/available on the system.
